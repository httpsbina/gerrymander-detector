Data and method

Partisan proxy: 2024 presidential race

I used the Harris vs. Trump results to measure how each precinct leans.

Two constraints: population and contiguity

The ensemble requires contiguous districts and population balance within +/-0.1%.

Mismatched boundaries

The final pipeline uses Census-block population rather than precinct-centroid population assignment. Block records are aggregated to the validated 2024 precinct geography. The final precinct dataset contains 9,712 units, including 322 zero-population units, and preserves the exact statewide population total.

Sample size

I reported 30,000 post-burn-in states. Effective sample sizes are approximately 98 for seat count, 92 for efficiency gap, and 118 for mean-median.

Limitations

I no longer have this earlier chain file, and I keep its reported values separate from the source rebuild. The states are autocorrelated, while descriptive tails, including zero counts, leave event probabilities unresolved. I used modeled presidential votes and population/contiguity constraints, with uniform sampling, compactness, county preservation, and full legal compliance outside the implemented model.
