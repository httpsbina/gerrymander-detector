Earlier Texas analysis

I retain this 30,000-state report as a historical record; the repository contains the source rebuild and continued baseline and dispersed-start audits.

Reported results

I reported 30,000 post-burn-in states with 38 graph-contiguous districts and population within ±0.1% of ideal. Using the 2024 Harris-Trump presidential proxy, I recorded a range of 9 to 17 Harris-majority districts; PLANC2333 scored 8, below that earlier observed range. The older map scored 11, with a descriptive seat lower tail of 12.607%.

Data and method

I aggregated Census-block population to the 2024 precinct geography, retaining 9,712 units and 322 zero-population units with the exact statewide total. I used modeled presidential votes to score partisan outcomes and constrained the sampler by population and graph contiguity.

I reported approximate effective sample sizes of 98 for seats, 92 for efficiency gap and 118 for mean-median.

Limitations

The earlier chain file is unavailable, so I cannot revalidate these reported values. The states are autocorrelated and the tail counts, including zero observations, leave event probabilities unresolved. Modeled presidential votes and population/contiguity constraints leave compactness, county preservation and other legal requirements outside the implemented model; I have not established uniform sampling or full legal compliance.
