Continuing the Texas baseline

I froze the NC baseline after its joint and starting-map screens passed; Texas is still running, though.

I retain the four baseline and four dispersed-start Texas chains, checking every primary metric within each cohort and jointly. I continue those chains under the recorded sampler and constraints until they meet the freeze criteria.

I generate paper figures, tables and findings from registered frozen outputs. When Texas freezes, the same export reads its registered inputs; the numerical record stays with the figures and tables.

I also retain complete CSV segments and matching checkpoints so I can continue on another machine. I check replay there and record any runtime boundary before adding a new segment.

Limitations

The convergence runs keep the sampler and constraints fixed. I assess data and model sensitivity in separate experiments.
