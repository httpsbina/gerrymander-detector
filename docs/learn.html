Reading and references

I keep these links alongside the graph and sampling methods used in the project.

I compare how district assignments change the grouping of voters while holding the experiment's inputs fixed. Packing describes concentrating a party's voters in a smaller set of districts, while cracking describes dividing them across districts; I use the sampled comparison to examine outcomes under my recorded rules.
