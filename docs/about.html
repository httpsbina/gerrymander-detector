About this project

I'm Bina, an undergraduate at Northeastern University's D'Amore-McKim School of Business. I study congressional redistricting through PEAK Award research advised by Dr. Eric Gerber.

I use GerryChain's ReCom sampler to compare Texas and North Carolina congressional maps, with data provenance, configurations, and numerical results recorded in the repository.

I conduct nonpartisan research with data from the Redistricting Data Hub.
